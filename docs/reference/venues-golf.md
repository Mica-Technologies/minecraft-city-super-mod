# Sports & Venues - Golf

A golf course, a driving range and mini-golf, decor without ball physics: turf in five cuts whose mowing stripes line up across blocks, the cup and a flagstick in four colours, raked bunker sand, tee markers, yardage markers, a tee sign with the hole, par and yards typed in, a ball washer, a bench, the cart path and its curb; a hitting mat, the bay divider, a ball dispenser, a bucket of balls and distance flags; mini-golf carpet in sixteen colours, the cup, a border along any side of a block, a ramp, a windmill, a loop and a scorecard stand.
