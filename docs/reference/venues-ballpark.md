# Sports & Venues - Ballpark

Baseball and softball: turf in two mowing tones, infield clay, packing clay and the warning track, the grass edges and chalk lines that run at 45 degrees across a diamond laid along the grid, the marking anchors and painted cells that draw the home plate area, base cutouts, coach's boxes, on-deck circles, the infield arc and softball's circle, the pitcher's mound, home plate, the bases and the rubber; the dugout's bench, rail, canopy, helmet and bat racks and water cooler; backstop and batting cage nets; the outfield wall with its distance markers and the foul pole; the line score scoreboard, the count board and a pitching machine.
