# Utilities

The services a city runs to its buildings: electric meters single and in banks, the meter socket, service disconnect, main breaker panel and switchboard, gas meters single and in banks, the water meter setter and utility room labels; and the water system: the water tower built to size (legs, riser, bracing, the pedestal column, tanks with their balcony and name band, the caged ladder) and ground storage tanks.
