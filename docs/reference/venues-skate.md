# Sports & Venues - Skate Park

Skate park basics in smooth concrete: quarter pipes of 1 m and 2 m, a bowl corner that turns two quarter pipes round an inside corner, a kicker, a bank and a box top for a funbox, a grind rail and a ledge that join into runs. The curves are drawn true; the collision is a staircase under them that a player can walk up.
