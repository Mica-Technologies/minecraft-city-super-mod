# Sports & Venues - Football & Soccer

American football and soccer at full size, one block a metre: field turf in two mowing tones, white paint and the surround, the end zone in any team colour, and the marking anchors and painted cells that draw every yard line, hash mark, number, arrow and pitch line where it really falls; the end zone lettering; the football goal post, the soccer goal and a small-sided goal; end zone pylons, team benches, a sideline heater and fan, the down box and chain set, corner flags, the substitutes' shelter and the fourth official's board; and the football and soccer scoreboards.
