# Sports & Venues - Courts, Track & Pool

Courts, a running track and a competition pool, one block a metre, every line where it really falls (worked out per cell, at the true radii on a track's bends): acrylic courts in sixteen colours for tennis, pickleball (alone or over tennis in a second line colour), basketball full and half court, volleyball and sand volleyball, laid from an anchor and painted cells; the tennis, pickleball and volleyball nets, the umpire chair, players' benches, a chain-link fence with windscreen, a divider curtain, a ball machine, the courtside scoreboard and a pole-mounted hoop; a World Athletics 400 m track in polyurethane with its staggers, relay zones, hurdle marks and lane numbers, the jumps and throws, hurdles, starting blocks, the finish camera tower, a lap counter and bell, the high jump and pole vault landing areas, the shot put toe board, the discus and hammer cage and the steeplechase barrier; a 25 m or 50 m pool round real water: tile, deck and a deck-level gutter, the floor's lane lines, the end walls' targets and touch pads, lane ropes at the water line, backstroke flags, numbered starting blocks, a lifeguard chair, ladders, 1 m and 3 m springboards and a 5 m platform, a pace clock and the scoreboard.
