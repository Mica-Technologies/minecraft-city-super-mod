# Sports & Venues - Bowling

A ten-pin bowling centre, one block a metre: lanes and approaches in maple or synthetic laid end to end to any length, a lane two blocks wide, that mark themselves (the foul line, the dots, the targeting arrows, the range finders, the pin deck and the pit) and raise bumpers on a sneak-click; the ten pins, knocked down by a click; the pinsetter and masking unit with four lit graphics; the ball return; settees that join round curved corners and the scorer's console; the overhead scoring monitors with a full ten-frame score for every bowler; the house ball rack; the shoe counter, cubbies and shoe spray.
