# Emergency Services & Fire Protection

Fire stations, police stations, ambulance stations and the fire protection in every building,
from the **CSM: Life Safety** module. Alongside **Fire Alarm & Detection** it has three creative
tabs: **Exits & Emergency Lighting**, **Fire Protection** and **Emergency Services**.

## Fire protection

The **Fire Protection** tab has what a building fights a fire with besides its alarm:

- **Fire extinguishers** on their wall brackets (ABC, CO2, water and class K), and **cabinets**
  for them. Click a cabinet to open or shut its door. The **AED cabinet** chirps when it is opened,
  as the real ones do.
- The **standpipe** (hose valve and riser), and **fire department connections**: brass and chrome
  wall connections, a freestanding one and a Storz inlet.
- The **riser room**: the sprinkler alarm valve, an OS&Y valve, the post indicator valve outside,
  a backflow preventer, and the **water motor gong** on the outside wall, which rings with the fire
  alarm's horns.
- The **Knox box**, **sprinkler heads**, and **sign plates** for all of them.
- **Magnetic door holders.** Put one beside a door, on the wall or the floor. It holds the door open
  until the fire alarm goes off, then lets it shut. Link it to a panel with the fire alarm linker,
  or leave it unlinked to follow any fire alarm sounding nearby. It works with any door.

The **Fire Alarm & Detection** tab gains photoelectric, duct and beam smoke detectors, and the
**remote annunciator**: it lights when its panel is in alarm, and right-clicking it tells you what
the panel says and where the first alarm came from.

The **Exits & Emergency Lighting** tab gains twin-head units, an LED bar, remote lamp heads, an
outdoor wall pack and a recessed downlight. Like the other emergency lights, redstone is mains
power: take it away and they light.

## Fire station

- Turnout gear lockers, the SCBA rack, cylinder cascade and **fill station**, hose racks, rolls and
  a drying rack, the nozzle rack, a tool board, the gear washer and an air compressor.
- **Fire pole.** Stack it from floor to floor, with the **floor opening** where it passes through
  a floor. Right-click the pole or the opening to grab it and slide down (it works while flying,
  too), or just step into the opening. Sneak to hold on. You take no fall damage at the bottom.
- The **firehouse gong** strikes its signal when it gets a redstone signal or is clicked.
- The **Maltese cross**, and the **station number plaque**: click to change the units digit, and
  sneak-click to change the tens, for any number from 0 to 99.
- **Station alerting.** Put the **controller** by the watch desk, and **speakers**, **alert
  lights**, **relays** and **bay clearance lights** around the station. Link them with the fire
  alarm linker: click the controller, then each device. Right-click the controller to choose a
  zone, and sneak-click with an empty hand to dispatch it, or give it a redstone signal. The
  speakers play the tones, the lights come on, the relays give redstone, and after ten seconds the
  bay clearance lights turn green. Wire a relay to a garage door opener and the bay doors open on
  a call, or to the gong and it rings.

## Police station

- The front desk counter, a deal tray and the lobby phone.
- **Walk-through metal detector.** Leave the block above it clear. Walk through carrying a weapon,
  a tool, metal armour or anything metal, and it beeps, turns red and gives a redstone pulse.
- **Holding cells.** The **cell door** is a block high; stack two. Click either one to slide the
  whole door open or shut, or hold it open with redstone. Also a bench, and a steel toilet and
  sink.
- Booking: the height chart, fingerprint scanner, camera and property bins. Evidence and equipment
  lockers, and a K-9 kennel.
- The **blue police lamp**, lit, and a police star.
- **Police and fire line tape.** Lay it a block at a time, like a fence. It joins other tape, the
  **tape stanchion**, and the side of any solid block. You can walk through it.

## Ambulance station

Stretchers (raised and lowered), a stair chair, backboards, supply shelving, oxygen cylinders, a
medication safe, a decontamination sink, an eyewash station and the Star of Life. The **first aid
cabinet** gives each player one first aid kit a day.

## Community warning and dispatch

- **Outdoor warning sirens**: the rotating siren, whose horn turns while it sounds, and an
  electronic array. Put them on top of a pole.
- The **siren controller**. Link its sirens with the fire alarm linker. Right-click to choose:
  alert, attack, fire, test, cancel, or the weekly test switch. Sneak-click with an empty hand to
  carry it out, or give it a redstone signal. With the weekly test on, the sirens sound a short
  test at noon every seventh day. A siren is heard across a town, and louder when its horn points
  your way.
- The **blue-light emergency call box**: press it to ring through.
- The **911 dispatch console**, a radio console speaker, and fallout shelter, storm shelter and
  assembly point signs.

## Items

- **Fire extinguisher.** Hold use to spray. It puts out fires and burning creatures in front of
  you, about five blocks out, for about ten seconds on a full charge. Use it on an **SCBA fill
  station** to recharge it. Sneak and use it on a wall to hang it there.
- **First aid kit.** Hold use to heal four hearts. Afterwards your kits rest for thirty seconds.

Both can be crafted in survival.
