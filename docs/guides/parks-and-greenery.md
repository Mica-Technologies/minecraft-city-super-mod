# Parks & Greenery

Street trees, plantings and park furniture, from the **CSM: Parks & Greenery** module. It has two
creative tabs: **Trees & Plants** and **Parks**.

## Trees are made of blocks

A CSM tree is built from log and leaves blocks, like a vanilla tree. You can build one by hand,
plant one with the **Tree Planting Tool**, or plant one and then edit it.

**Logs** come in every species' bark and five widths: twig, thin, medium, thick and full (a
vanilla-sized log). Logs join up by themselves:

- A log joins the logs, leaves and ground around it, and flares out where it stands on the ground.
- A wide log tapers into a narrower one above it.
- **To lean a trunk, step it diagonally**, the way vanilla acacias lean. Put each log one block up
  and one block over from the last. The logs bridge the step, so the trunk reads as one smooth,
  leaning trunk. Limbs that spread at 45 degrees work the same way.
- You can mix vanilla logs in.

**Leaves** are drawn as clusters of leaves, not cubes. Their edge reaches a little past the
block, so a crown looks like a crown, and a single column of cypress foliage looks like an Italian
cypress. Leaves:

- never decay;
- can be walked through, so a canopy over a sidewalk never blocks it;
- come in fixed colours for each species, the same in every biome. Several species also have an
  **autumn** set, and the jacaranda a **blossom** set: swap them in for the season.

**Palm crowns** go on top of a palm log. **Spanish Moss** and **Willow Strands** hang under a limb or
crown and stack downward into a curtain.

## The Tree Planting Tool

Right-click the ground to plant a whole tree of the selected species. **Sneak and right-click** to
change species.

The tree **leans and reaches the way you are facing**. Stand on the sidewalk, face the road and
plant, and the tree arches out over the road. Leaning trees keep their canopy above the street, so
nothing hangs into traffic. Each tree comes out a little different, so a planted row doesn't look
copy-pasted.

If anything is in the way (a building, a pole, another tree), nothing is planted, and the tool tells
you how many blocks are in the way. Plant straight onto mulch, gravel or turf and the trunk goes
through it to the ground. Everything it plants is ordinary blocks, so trim or reshape the
tree afterwards if you like.

!!! tip "A hedge on stilts"

    Pleached lindens are clipped into a box that is wide across the way you face. Plant a row of
    them five blocks apart, all facing the same way, and the boxes join into one long clipped hedge
    on bare trunks, the European street style.

## Around street trees

- **Tree grates** (square and round) and the **mulched tree pit** replace the ground block under a
  tree. The trunk stands on them.
- The **Tree Pit Hoop Fence** goes round a pit and joins up like a fence.
- A **Tree Stake** stands next to a young tree, with its tie reaching over to the trunk; it turns
  to the trunk by itself when you place it. Place one on each side.
- **Hanging baskets** hang on a bracket from a wall or pole, and fit any traffic pole's thickness.

## Plantings

- **Hedges** in boxwood and privet, low and tall, join like fences.
- **Shrubs** (boxwood ball, hydrangea, upright juniper) and **ornamental grasses**.
- **Flower beds**: low bedding flowers on mulch, in four colours.
- **Ground covers**: mulch, pea gravel, decomposed granite and artificial turf, each a thin layer
  you lay on the ground.
- **Planters** in concrete, cedar and corten steel. **Raised beds** join into one bed of any shape,
  walled only round the outside.

## The Parks tab

- **Benches and picnic tables.** Place them side by side for a longer one: the legs and arms appear
  only at the two ends. Right-click one to **sit down**, and sneak to get up. A bench seats one
  person a block. A picnic table seats one on each side: you sit on the bench on your side,
  facing across the table.
- **Bins**: a recycling bin, a trash and recycling station, the park trash can and a dog waste
  station.
- **Playground**: the slide, spring rider, swings and teeter totter, and a rubber safety surface to
  go under them.
- **A pergola**: stand pergola posts at the corners, then fill the top with pergola beams at any
  size. The beams run round the outside and the rafters across.
- **A gazebo**: lay a floor of gazebo deck, stand gazebo posts 3 high at the corners (and at the
  middle of each side for a big one), and run gazebo railings between them, leaving a gap for the
  way in. Then put a **Gazebo Roof** on top of the middle, level with the post tops: the 3x3 roof
  covers a 3 by 3 gazebo, the 5x5 a 5 by 5.
- **Fountains.** Fountain basins join into a pool of any size. Stand a tiered fountain in the middle
  and the pool runs right up to it. There are also water bubblers, a bird bath and flower pots.
- **Irrigation.** The **Irrigation Controller** is a wall box that sends out a redstone signal
  from 5 to 7 in the morning. Right-click it to switch the manual override on or off. Run redstone
  from it past **Pop-Up Sprinklers**: each one rises and sweeps a jet of water while the line
  beside it is powered. A **Backflow Preventer** finishes the job.

## Survival

Everything here is made in the **CSM Fabricator**. It takes what a thing is made of:

| Block | Costs |
|---|---|
| Logs | Planks, more for a wider log |
| Leaves | Vanilla leaves |
| Plantings | Saplings, grass or flowers |
| Wooden furniture | Planks |
| Metal pieces | Sheet metal and fasteners |

The Tree Planting Tool is crafted from an iron shovel, a sapling and bone meal.
