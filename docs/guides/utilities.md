# Utilities

The services a city runs, from the meter on a building's wall back to the plant: building
service meters, the water system, sewer and stormwater, a gas regulator yard and telecom. It is
the **Utilities** tab of the **CSM: Utilities** module (which also holds the [Power
Grid](power-grid.md) tab). The module needs **CSM: Roads & Traffic**: its larger pieces are the
same one-unit boxes as the Streetscape tab's.

Nothing in the tab ticks or needs a renderer, so a whole pump station costs no more each frame
than the blocks it is built from.

## Building service meters

Electric meters (digital and analog), a blank meter socket, the service disconnect, the main
breaker panel and a service entrance switchboard; gas meters; the water meter setter; and labels
for the utility room door. They hang on the wall you are looking at.

- **Meter banks join.** Place electric or gas meter banks side by side and they become one bank,
  with the end pieces only at its ends (the gas bank's supply riser and regulator at one end).
- **Right-click the main panel** to open its door and see the breakers.
- The meter faces run: the digital display cycles, the analog disk turns, the gas and water
  registers count.

## The water system

**Water towers are built to size.** Stack legs and a riser (they grow a concrete pier at the
bottom), add struts and cross bracing between the legs, and hang a caged ladder on a leg. Or stack
pedestal sections for a single-column tower. Then place a **tank** on top: on the riser's top for
a lattice tower, on the column's top for a pedestal tower. The tank places whole, in two sizes of
each kind, with its balcony and a name band.

- The small lattice tank wants its legs 3 blocks out from the riser, the medium one 5 blocks out;
  the tank draws the tops of its legs so you can see where they go.
- **Right-click a tank** to change the town on its band (sneak to go back).
- Breaking any part of a tank removes the whole tank.

**Ground storage tanks** stack a layer at a time; place a layer on a layer of the same size to
make it taller.

**The pump station**: water pipe that joins on every side, gate, butterfly and check valves, a
flow meter and an air release valve that sit in a pipe run, split-case and vertical inline pumps,
a pressure tank, and a control panel. Pumps and the pressure tank have pipe connections a run
joins. Outside: the air release valve enclosure and vault, and the backflow preventer's hot box.
For treatment, a chemical feed skid and a chlorine cylinder scale.

## Sewer and stormwater

- **Access hatches** for a wet well or a valve vault go **in the ground**: dig the hole first and
  place the hatch in it. It places both leaves; right-click to open. A wet well keeps its safety
  grate shut under the open leaves.
- **The lift station control panel** alarms when it gets **redstone**: its beacon flashes and its
  flood light comes on.
- **The standby generator**: right-click to start or stop it.
- **Curb inlets** set in the sidewalk's edge, facing the road; side by side they make one long
  inlet. Put Streetscape's gutter inlet grate in the road in front for a combination inlet.
- **The outfall**: headwall blocks join into one wall, with a pipe mouth or a pipe with its flap
  gate, wingwalls and riprap.
- **The detention pond**: an outlet riser that stacks, and an emergency spillway crest.
- **Manholes**: riser sections and a cone, stacked, with Streetscape's manhole cover on top. The
  **cutaway** sections show the inside from the side.

## Gas yard and telecom

- **Gas pipe** is smaller and yellow, and joins only other gas pieces, so a gas run and a water
  run side by side stay apart. Ball valve, pressure regulator and turbine meter sit in a run.
- **The regulator station skid** goes **into the ground**, its deck level with it; pipe stands
  appear under the run above it. A line heater, a vent stack and an odorant tank finish the yard.
- **Signs** (gas warning, no smoking, emergency contact, station name, authorised personnel, cell
  site) hang on a wall or on a chain-link fence.
- **The fibre distribution cabinet**: right-click to open its doors.
- **A cell site**: equipment and battery cabinets, an ice bridge on its stanchions, a GPS antenna,
  and a **monopole** stacked to any height with an **antenna array** placed in the stack.
- **Small cells**: a canister antenna on top of one of CSM's street poles and a radio on its side;
  both fit the pole they are on.
