# Furniture

Houses, offices and shops, from the **CSM: Furniture & Novelties** module. This page is about the
furniture that **does something**: pieces that join into runs, beds you sleep in, working
appliances, taps, TVs and a piano, a light switch you can link, and a store's refrigerated cases
and checkout. It covers three creative tabs: **Residential**, **Commercial & Office** and
**Market & Store**.

Most pieces come in several finishes, each its own block: oak, walnut and white wood; charcoal,
navy, oatmeal and red fabric; stainless or white appliances.

## Sitting down

Chairs, stools, armchairs, sofas, benches, office chairs, the school desk, the kiddie pool and the
sun lounger all seat you: **right-click** to sit and **sneak** to get up. A sneaking click never
sits you, so you can still place blocks against a chair.

## Pieces that join into runs

Place these side by side, facing the same way, and they draw as one piece, with legs, arms and end
panels only at the ends:

- **Dining tables**, **patio tables**, the **conference table** and the **trampoline** join on all
  four sides into any rectangle, with legs only at the outer corners.
- **Sofas** join into one long sofa, and a **sofa corner** of the same fabric turns it into an L.
  The outdoor sofa works the same way.
- **Bookcases** and **office shelving** also stack, sharing one top and one plinth.
- **Kitchen cabinets** of one finish join under **one countertop**, whatever their front (doors,
  drawers, sink), so a drawer bank and a sink base share one top. The **corner cabinet** turns a
  run; two corners make a U. Wall cabinets and islands join their own kind. The range, wall oven
  and dishwasher stand in a base run without breaking it, and the dishwasher carries the
  countertop of the cabinet beside it.
- **Bathroom vanities** join under one stone top, a basin and tap in every block.
- **Office desks** join into one desktop; the **L-desk corner** turns the run. The **reception
  desk** joins the same way.
- **Cubicle panels** join like a fence, with posts only at ends and corners. Put a half-height
  panel on a full one for a taller panel. Right-click a panel's face with an empty hand to hang a
  shelf on it, and again to take it down.
- **Whiteboards**, chalkboards and cork boards join into one long board.
- **Lockers**, wardrobes and closets join into a row.
- **Gondola shelving** in the store joins and stacks with any other gondola, whatever it's
  stocked with, so canned goods beside cereal over snacks is one run. Two runs back to back make an
  island. **End caps** close the end of a run.
- **Store counters** join into one lane or counter: the checkout pieces, the produce stand, the
  coffee station, the pharmacy counters, the clothes rails and more. **Rugs**, bath mats and
  **string lights** join too.

**Storage.** Many pieces hold items: TV stands (9 slots), sideboards (18), kitchen cabinets,
dressers, nightstands, the refrigerator (27), filing cabinets, lockers and more. Right-click to
open. Hoppers can put items in and take them out, and a comparator reads how full they are.
Breaking the piece drops what it held.

## Beds

Beds sleep like a vanilla bed: sleeping skips the night and sets your spawn point. There are
single, double and king beds in four fabrics, a bunk bed and a day bed.

- A double, a king or a bunk sleeps **two**: you get the side, or the bunk, you clicked.
- A bed places whole, only if every block it needs is free, and breaks whole.
- The **day bed** is a seat by day and a bed at night.
- In the Nether or the End a bed tells you it can't be slept in. It doesn't explode.

## Kitchen appliances

Working appliances all behave the same way:

1. **Right-click** to open the appliance's screen (it looks like a furnace's).
2. Put in what it takes. It **runs by itself** while its input holds something it can use. No
   power, fuel or redstone is needed, except for the charcoal grill.
3. Take the result from the output slot. Its window lights while it runs, and it sounds when done.

Hoppers can feed the input and take from the output, so a hopper-fed oven just cooks.

| Appliance | Takes | Makes | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Range** and **wall oven** | Anything that smelts into food | The cooked food | 8 s |
| **Microwave** | The same, one at a time | The cooked food | 3 s |
| **Air fryer** | Raw food: meat, fish, potatoes | The cooked food | 4 s |
| **Toaster** | Bread, two at a time | Toast | 5 s |
| **Blender** | An apple, or two melon slices | A smoothie | 3 s |
| **Coffee machine** | Cocoa beans, and water | Coffee (gives Speed) | 4 s |
| **Dishwasher** | A damaged tool or weapon, and water | The same, repaired a little each wash | 12 s a wash |

The dishwasher keeps washing until the tool is whole. It doesn't take armour; the washing machine
does.

**Water.** An appliance that uses water has a tank, shown as a gauge on its screen. Right-click it
with a **water bucket** to fill the tank, or a **water bottle** to add one cycle. Stand it **beside
a tap** (a kitchen sink, a vanity, a basin, the bathtub or the hose reel) and it's plumbed: it never
runs dry.

**Other kitchen pieces:**

- **Range hoods** and the **under-cabinet light**: right-click to switch on and off.
- The **refrigerator** is two blocks tall, placed and broken as one, with 27 slots.
- The **chest freezer** turns a water bottle into ice, and a water bucket into packed ice, after
  30 seconds inside.
- The **kettle** whistles and steams when clicked; the **stand mixer** whirrs.
- The **cookie jar** holds cookies and nothing else.
- Small pieces (the kettle, mixer, toaster, plates, mugs, a TV, a lamp) stand on whatever is under
  them: a countertop, a table, a coffee table, a desk or a nightstand. They sit at the right height
  on each.

## Taps, the bathtub and the shower

**Taps.** Hold an **empty bucket** or a **glass bottle** to a kitchen sink, a bathroom vanity, the
pedestal sink, the laundry tub, the bathtub or the hose reel, and it comes back full of water.
With anything else in your hand, a kitchen sink or a vanity opens the storage under it.

**The bathtub** is two blocks long.

- A **water bucket** fills it; an **empty bucket** scoops the water back out.
- Right-click **the tap end** with an empty hand to run the tap (or pull the plug).
- Right-click **the other end** with an empty hand to sit in the bath.

**The shower** enclosure (two blocks tall) and the wall **shower head**: right-click to turn the
spray on or off. Walk into the enclosure through its open side and stand under it.

**The toilet**: click the seat to sit, and the cistern (the back of the block) to flush. The
**urinal** flushes on any click. The **baby changing station** folds down and up on a click.

## Laundry

The **washing machine** and **dryer** work like the kitchen appliances: right-click to open, put
something in, and they run by themselves. Both windows light while they run.

| Machine | Takes | Makes | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Washing machine** | A piece of damaged armour, and water | The same, repaired a little each wash, until it is whole | 12 s a wash |
| **Dryer** | Wet sponges | Dry sponges | 10 s |

A washing machine beside the laundry tub is plumbed and never runs dry. The **steam iron** stands on
the ironing board and hisses and puffs steam when clicked.

## TV, stereo and piano

- **TVs** (flat screens on a stand or the wall, one or two blocks wide, and a tube TV): right-click
  to change channel through off, news, sports, nature, colour bars and snow. On a two-block TV,
  clicking either half changes both.
- **The stereo**: right-click with a **music disc** to put it on the turntable and play it, as a
  jukebox does. Right-click again to take it out. A comparator reads the disc as it would a
  jukebox's.
- **The upright piano**: click a key to play it, two octaves, low notes on your left. The black
  keys are at the back of the keyboard. The **piano bench** faces the keys.
- **Clocks**: the digital clock and the wall clock both show the world's time.

## Lamps, fires and fans

- **Lamps** (floor and table lamps, desk lamps, kitchen lights, candles) switch on a click, or
  follow redstone: on when power comes, off when it goes.
- **The ceiling fan** runs on redstone: powered, the light is on and the blades turn. Between
  changes of power, a click steps it through off, fan, fan and light, and light.
- **The fireplace** and **fire pits**: light them with flint and steel, or right-click with an empty
  hand to light them or put them out; redstone works too. The fire is only drawn: it sets nothing
  alight. The **chimney** stacks, and lights the whole stack at once.
- **The doorbell** chimes and gives a one-second redstone pulse, like a button.

## The light switch

The **light switch** works as a lever on the wall it's placed on. It can also be **linked** to one
block anywhere nearby, so it can switch a lamp across the room without any wiring:

1. Hold the switch and right-click the block it should control: a CSM lamp, a vanilla redstone
   lamp, a door, trapdoor, fence gate, piston, dispenser, note block, powered rail and so on.
   A linked switch shimmers, and its tooltip shows where it's linked.
2. Place the switch anywhere **within 32 blocks** of that block, in the same dimension.
3. Flip it: the linked block switches.

To clear a link before placing, sneak and right-click the air or any other block with the switch.
To check a placed switch, sneak and right-click it with an empty hand, and it says what it's linked
to.

## Outdoors

- **Patio umbrellas**: right-click to open or furl. The table umbrella goes on a patio table.
- **Grills** cook like the oven. The gas grill needs only food; the charcoal grill also burns
  furnace fuel from its third slot.
- **The trampoline**, the **bounce castle** floor and the **diving board** cancel fall damage and
  bounce you back up. Jump each time you land to bounce higher. Sneak to land without bouncing.
- **The bounce castle** is three blocks square and places whole, with its doorway facing you.
- **The picket gate** opens like a fence gate: a click or redstone.
- **The hose reel** has a working tap.

## The copier

The **copier** in the Commercial & Office tab copies **written books**. Put the book in the input
slot and a **book and quill** in the supply slot. Each copy uses one book and quill and takes 5
seconds, and the original stays in the input. The crafting table's rules apply: an original makes
a copy of the original, which makes a copy of a copy, which can't be copied. A written book doesn't
stack, so the copier makes one copy and waits for it to be taken, unless a hopper under it takes
each one as it comes.

In the office, the desk lamp, the computer screens and the laptop also switch on and off with a
click, and the projector screen comes down or goes up.

## The store

### Refrigerated displays

The glass-door **reach-in coolers and freezers**, the **open dairy case**, the **floral cooler**,
the **island freezer**, the **ice cream cabinet**, the **deli**, **butcher** and **seafood cases**,
the **bakery case** and the **hot food case** all light up when placed.

- A **sneaking right-click with an empty hand** switches the lights. A plain right-click opens the
  case's storage.
- **Redstone** switches them too: on when power comes, off when it goes. A linked light switch
  works them.
- One switch reaches the displays **joined to it, up to five** in all. A switch at the end of a row
  works that case and the next four; one in the middle works two either way. A longer row needs a
  second switch.
- The deli, butcher and seafood cases join one another in any order, as one service counter.

### Sale tags

Right-click a **gondola** or **end cap** with an empty hand to hang **SALE tags** under its price
strips (yellow price tags on an end cap). Click again to add a **shelf talker** that sticks out into
the aisle, and a third time to take both off. Sneak and right-click to step back.

### The checkout

- The **belt counter**, **scanner counter**, **bagging end** and **candy strip** join into one lane.
  The belt moves, and the scanner beeps when clicked. The bagging end holds 9 slots.
- **Registers** (a POS terminal and an old brass register) have a nine-slot drawer: right-click to
  open it.
- The **self-checkout** beeps when clicked. A card terminal, the Verifone MX915 and a receipt
  printer stand on the counter.
- The **coffee brewer**, the **fountain drink machine** (sugar and water make a fountain drink) and
  the **rotisserie oven** (raw meat and fish) work like the kitchen appliances.

### Fitting rooms and signs

- **Fitting rooms** are two-block booths that join into a row. Click either half to draw the
  curtain across or back. An open booth can be walked into.
- **The aisle sign**: right-click to step its number 1 to 16, sneak and right-click to go back.
- **Department signs**: right-click to step through the departments (Produce, Bakery, Deli and
  more), sneak and right-click to go back.
