WHICH JAR THIS APPLIES TO

  Every component listed below ships in exactly one jar: the Text to Speech
  module, minecraft-city-super-mod-tts-<version>.jar. It carries the MaryTTS
  speech engine, its four voices, and the libraries MaryTTS needs that
  Minecraft and Forge do not provide — ICU4J, Apache Commons Lang, Apache
  Commons Collections, the Log4j 1.2 API bridge and SLF4J. Those five are
  embedded via the Gradle shadow plugin and relocated under
  com.micatechnologies.minecraft.csm.shadow, so they cannot collide with the
  copies Minecraft and Forge load; MaryTTS itself and the voices keep their own
  package names. The same jar also carries the remaining libraries MaryTTS
  depends on, under their own licenses.

  No other jar in a release bundles any third-party code. The Core jar
  (minecraft-city-super-mod-core-<version>.jar) and every subsystem module jar
  — Roads & Traffic, Life Safety, HVAC, Lighting, Power Grid, Technology,
  Furniture & Novelties, Building Materials — contain only this mod's own
  classes and assets. Nothing in this file applies to them, and a player who
  does not install the Text to Speech jar receives none of it.
